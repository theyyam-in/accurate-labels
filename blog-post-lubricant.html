Lubricant and Engine Oil Labeling Standards in the UAE

Industrial fluids and lubricants are exposed to some of the most extreme environments imaginable. From drastic temperature changes to constant exposure to harsh chemicals, your labeling needs to be as robust as the product itself. Ensuring compliance and durability is not just a regulatory hurdle; it is crucial for brand integrity and consumer safety.

Regulatory Compliance in the UAE

In the UAE, standardizing safety protocols is paramount. Labels for engine oils, hydraulic fluids, and industrial lubricants must clearly display GHS (Globally Harmonized System) hazard warnings, batch numbers, volume metrics, and handling instructions. Failing to meet these standards can result in severe penalties and product recalls.

Material and Adhesive Selection

Because engine oil containers often experience leakage, friction, and high temperatures, the label material must be oil-resistant and heat-resistant. BOPP (Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene) combined with a heavy-duty, permanent adhesive is the industry standard. These materials prevent the label from peeling, curling, or bubbling when exposed to the fluids inside the container or extreme warehouse heat.

Advanced Printing Techniques

To ensure that critical safety information remains legible over years of storage, UV-resistant inks and protective overlaminates are used. This ensures that even if a barrel is left outdoors in the harsh UAE sun, the warning labels and brand imagery will not fade or scratch off.
