Food Label Requirements UAE: A Comprehensive Guide to Compliance

The United Arab Emirates maintains some of the highest standards for food safety and consumer protection in the Middle East. For businesses manufacturing or importing food products, understanding the complex regulatory landscape managed by the **Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology (MoIAT)** is essential for market entry and brand longevity.

Mandatory Branding and Language

The most critical requirement in the UAE is **Arabic labeling**. According to standard UAE.S GSO 9:2017, all mandatory information must be presented in Arabic. While bilingual labels (Arabic/English) are common and recommended, the Arabic text must be accurate and must not be smaller than the English counterpart.

Essential Label Elements

Every prepackaged food item must clearly display specific data points to remain compliant with federal law:

  • Product Name & Description: Clearly reflecting the nature of the food.
  • List of Ingredients: Displayed in descending order of weight.
  • Net Content: Using the metric system (g, kg, ml, L).
  • Country of Origin: Explicitly stated.
  • Date Marking: Both production and expiry dates in DD/MM/YYYY format.

Nutritional Information and Claims

Since 2021, nutritional labeling has become even more stringent (UAE.S GSO 2233:2021). You must include energy, protein, carbohydrates, fats, and sodium levels. Furthermore, specialized claims like "Halal," "Organic," or "Gluten-Free" require specific recognized certifications to be displayed on the final label.

Compliance Checkpoint: The ZAD System

All food products must be registered in the UAE's national **ZAD system** before they can be sold. Accurate Labels Manufacturing helps you ensure your physical labels match the digital requirements for a smooth registration process.
